# cybersecurity-basics-2025
Tips to help small businesses improve cybersecurity and avoid common risks.

Cyber threats are a growing problem for businesses of every size. But small companies often assume they’re too small to matter. In reality, attackers don’t care how big you are — they care how unprepared you are.
Here are five common gaps we see, and how to close them:

1. Relying Too Heavily on Cloud Services
Cloud platforms are useful, but they don’t come fully secured out of the box. Settings like “anyone with the link” can unintentionally share sensitive data.

Try this:
Audit your file-sharing settings, use login protection with a code, and remove access when people leave the team.

2. Backups That Haven’t Been Checked
Saving files isn’t enough — you need to know you can actually get them back.

Try this:
Run a quick file restore every month or so. Keep a separate copy in a location that’s not always connected to your network.

3. No Security Training for Your Team
Even well-meaning employees can fall for realistic-looking emails or login pages.

Try this:
Share quick tips on what fake messages look like. Encourage staff to ask questions when they’re unsure — it’s better to ask than click.

4. Trusting Outside Software Without Checking It
Many businesses use external tools for payroll, marketing, or scheduling. If one of them gets compromised, it could affect your business too.

Try this:
Stick with trusted tools. Ask vendors about their approach to data protection before sharing access.

5. Putting Off System Updates
Every day you delay an update, you’re giving cybercriminals more time to exploit known flaws.

Try this:
Enable automatic updates where you can.

Final Word
Cybersecurity doesn’t have to be complicated. Focus on a few good habits, and you’ll be ahead of most businesses in your space.
